Commit Message:

Bugfix: Allow one WebUI random pick at a time with a 10-second timeout

- Random picks go through one picker per page: a press, swipe, or auto-advance while a pick is waiting sends nothing, so repeated presses on a slow or stalled connection can no longer play and record several items at once when the answers arrive.
- A pick the server does not answer within 10 seconds is cancelled through its fetch signal, shows "No response from the server. Try again.", and relays a `playback=random-pick-timeout` line with `timeoutMs`; the next press starts a fresh request, and the cancelled pick's answer is never used even if it arrives after a newer pick was sent.
- Every other answer keeps its status message: unauthorized, a failed request, no eligible media, and a network error.
- Tests cover the one-at-a-time guard, the timeout and abort, a late answer after a newer pick, and the answer mapping, and each fails when its guard is removed.
- WebUI Status Line Overhaul notes that the playing video's status can hide "Loading..." and the timeout message, and Release Specific has a manual check with the server paused.
