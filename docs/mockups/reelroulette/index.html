ReelRoulette design mockup

The app's whole visible UI, approved before the v0.15.0 milestones that build it and kept matching the app as each one ships. Every page works at desktop and phone widths, from about 425 px up, below which the player's transport row would wrap, and follows the system light or dark theme. Magenta dashed boxes are mockup notes. Every screen here is approved, and each decision is recorded in the milestone that builds it.

dock_to_rightMain page

  • Nothing plays until a tap or Play starts a random pick. Previous and Next step through what played, and Next, R, and autoplay pick at random at its end. Each skips a missing file or one this browser can't play, saying so in a popup.
  • No status line. Lasting states (reconnecting, a server that fails the compatibility check or runs without a library, loading, and a library refresh) show one at a time on the player's indicator. One-off messages pop up briefly, three at most. The bell at the player's top left, which shows and hides with its other controls, lists recent messages, the in-app notices included, and every lasting state under Now; its badge counts what didn't pop up.
  • The player's one panel button opens the panel on its last tab, and closes it. It sits on the panel's side, at the top right, or just right of the bell while the panel is on the left. Favorite and Blacklist stay on the player; the seek row ends with Mute and the volume slider.
  • The player's controls don't appear, hide, or move on their own; a control may hide only because of a user setting, as the volume slider does on iOS while Enhanced audio is off.
  • Drag the handle between the player and the panel, or focus it and use the arrow keys. Open or closed, side, width, and last tab survive a reload.
  • Below 800 px wide, or on a touch screen under 500 px tall, the panel is a full-screen overlay. On a phone on its side the header also hides and the player fills the screen. The chip at the player's top left shows what Auto-Pause did.
  • Swipe left or right on the player for the next or previous item. Favorite and Blacklist clear each other.
  • Ambient mode: a blurred, dimmed copy of the picture's colors fills the black space around it, drifting as the video plays. The Settings tab turns it off, and its Ambient mode settings set the update rate, fade time, blur, strength in each theme, and saturation.
  • Volume: the scroll wheel over the player and a two-finger drag on a touch screen change it by the volume step, and so do [ and ]; each shows the volume indicator, centered over the player, which the slider doesn't. Mute moves the slider to 0 and unmuting brings the volume back. While muted, raising the volume unmutes and starts from 0, and lowering it does nothing. Lowering it to 0 mutes, and unmuting then brings back the volume from before that drag, notch, or key press. With Enhanced audio, on by default in Settings, every device plays through a Web Audio gain stage, started on the first tap, so iOS gets a volume slider too. The Mockup tab's stand-in sound plays with videos so the volume can be heard, and the Mockup tab can make the gain stage fail to show its notice. With Loudness normalization on, the stand-in sound follows each video's loudness against the baseline.
  • Autoplay has two modes in Settings: Normal, where photos advance after Advance after and videos play to the end, and Timer, where every item advances after it, a paused video holding the timer. With Loop on, the item repeats and nothing advances while Loop stops autoplay is on, its default; in Timer mode a video then repeats a stretch of Advance after seconds, from its start or from where a seek lands, shown faintly on the scrub bar. Turned off, autoplay goes on with Loop on, and a video shorter than Advance after repeats until it advances: at once in Timer mode, and once the repeat in progress finishes in Normal mode. A repeat never loads the item again.
  • On a photo that autoplay will advance, Play or Space pauses and resumes its timer, and dragging the scrub bar moves it. Otherwise both are disabled on a photo.
  • Library: controls that collapse, the filter summary line, the playing tile's icon, the favorite and blacklist icons, placeholder tiles while a page loads, and file names on tiles as a setting. Selection works as in Google Photos: start it with the check at a tile's top left when hovering it, or a long press on a touch screen; then a click adds or removes a tile, Shift+click adds a range, and deselecting the last tile ends it. Edit tags opens the Tags tab on the selected items.
  • Filter, Tags, and their dialogs as approved so far, with Favorites and Blacklisted each set to only or excluded, plus the browser-playable option. Pick an .avi or .wmv tile to see the format notice.
  • Tag categories start collapsed and remember which are open, separately in the Tags tab and the Filter tab. A category's header, expanded or collapsed, shows how many of its tags are in use and, with unsaved changes, the orange dot; adding a tag to a category, or moving one into it, opens it.
  • Auto Tag opens from the Tags tab over the whole page. Its scan runs for about 5 seconds, then lists the tags file names match, and its own Apply applies the checked changes.
  • Stats: the current file, with Show in File Manager or Copy Path, and the library. Click the file name in the header to open it. A video's Adjustment shows what loudness normalization applies, with “(limited by peak)” when the video's peak holds a boost back, or Off while it or Enhanced audio is off. True peak is the loudness scan's, Not measured, or N/A, and Last played is the time before this play. The Mockup tab's Stats load shows a failed load.
  • Settings, in sections: Playback (autoplay mode, Advance after, Loop stops autoplay, seek step), Audio (volume step, Enhanced audio, Loudness normalization with its target, boost limit, and Allow limiting, and Compression with its strength, Light, Medium, Strong, or Custom with its values), Player controls (when they hide), Appearance (theme, ambient mode and its settings, file names on tiles), Notifications (whether popups show, and under Popup settings, for which kinds, where, whether in fullscreen, and for how long), Panel (side, Auto-Pause), Keyboard shortcuts (collapsed; ? opens it), and Diagnostics. Checkboxes, radio buttons, and sliders use the orange accent.
  • Keyboard focus shows in orange everywhere. In the Library, Tab enters the grid once, the arrow keys move between tiles, Tab from a tile reaches its check icon, Space or Enter toggles it, and Tab again leaves the grid.
  • Keyboard shortcuts work: ? (shows them), K or Space, J, L, arrows, R, F, B, A, M, comma and period (one frame, while paused), [ and ] (volume), 1 to 5, T, S, P, and Shift+F.
  • The Mockup tab on the left edge sets the situation: the server reconnecting, not answering, running without a library, failing the compatibility check, or with an empty library; pairing asked for (a token of "wrong" fails); the server machine; a read-only volume; and a phone on its side. It also starts and ends each lasting state, sends a test notification of each kind, a favorite and preset changes from another device, and a runtime config failure, and can hide every mockup note to show screens as they will ship.

admin_panel_settingsAdmin view

  • The full-page view the header's admin icon opens: server and updates, web access, control, refresh, library transfer, backups, sources, duplicates, the testing suite, clients, the Log Viewer, and events. Daily backups start off, at 0 days, and the restore list shows daily backups only once they're on. An import or a restore first backs up the current library, listed first as "(before import)" or "(before restore)".
  • Duplicate review opens within it. The control token gate shows first from another machine.
  • It has no player, so its bell is at the end of its own bar, which stays on a phone on its side; its acknowledgments pop up, and Refresh Now shows its stage and progress in its own card.
  • Refresh Status, the import's upload progress, and its notice for a file that isn't a library. The mockup bar also shows a dev run's version line and a phone on its side, where the admin bar stays.

restart_altRecovery page

  • The server's own plain page for when the WebUI's files are missing or broken: restart, stop, updates, and the newest log lines, behind the control token from another machine.

errorField validation pattern

  • Each state of the durations (parsed strictly), the new preset name, and the Edit Tag and Edit Category names, with what a screen reader gets.
  • Apply when the field that is not valid is out of view.

Library tilesRows 100–240 px, aiming for 160.
PopupsAt most 3 at once, each for 3, 5 (the default), or 10 seconds.
Notifications listThe last 50 messages, for the page's life.
Player indicatorLoading… after half a second. Below the corner buttons on a player under 600 px wide; above that, at most the player's width less 336 px, so it clears them.
