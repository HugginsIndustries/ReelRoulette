ReelRoulette design mockup

The app's whole visible UI, approved before the v0.15.0 milestones that build it and kept matching the app as each one ships. Every page works at desktop and phone widths, from about 425 px up, below which the player's transport row would wrap, and follows the system light or dark theme. Magenta dashed boxes are mockup notes. Every screen here is approved, and each decision is recorded in the milestone that builds it.

dock_to_rightMain page

  • Nothing plays until a tap or Play starts a random pick. Previous and Next step through what played, and Next and autoplay pick at random at its end. R and the Random button pick from anywhere in it. Each skips a missing file or one this browser can't play, saying so in a popup. Play behavior, in Settings, places a pick from R or the Random button, and a file played from the library, right after the current item with Insert, its default, or at the end with Jump; either way every entry stays.
  • No status line. Lasting states (reconnecting, a newer build of ReelRoulette on the server, a server that fails the compatibility check or runs without a library, loading, and a library refresh) show one at a time on the player's indicator. One-off messages pop up briefly, three at most. The bell at the player's top left, which shows and hides with its other controls, lists recent messages, the in-app notices included, and every lasting state under Now; its badge counts what didn't pop up, and the bell is orange while there is any. Each message shows its time, "HH:MM" today and "MM-DD HH:MM" for another day, and the list is kept on the device across reloads, with what was read, until Clear.
  • A running library refresh shows its stage and percent on the indicator, and under Now its progress line too, such as "Checking 12,000 of 49,455 files". A finished one pops up only what it changed, "No changes" when nothing did, and a failed one the stage it stopped in. An automatic refresh with no library changes goes only to the list, even one that names problems such as "ffmpeg not found", and the badge doesn't count it.
  • When the server serves a newer build, "ReelRoulette updated · Tap to reload" shows on the indicator and under Now, except while reconnecting, since a reload then would fail. A tap on either reloads the page, after asking "Discard changes?" while the Filter or Tags tab holds unsaved changes. With a server that also fails the compatibility check, the indicator shows the update and Now lists both.
  • The player's one panel button opens the panel on its last tab, and closes it. It sits on the panel's side, at the top right, or just right of the bell while the panel is on the left. The rating's star, Favorite, and Blacklist stay on the player; the seek row ends with Mute and the volume slider. The panel's tabs are, in order, Library, Tags, Stats, Filter, Randomization, and Settings.
  • The player's controls don't appear, hide, or move on their own; a control may hide only because of a user setting, as the volume slider does on iOS while Enhanced audio is off.
  • Drag the handle between the player and the panel. Open or closed, side, width, and last tab survive a reload.
  • Below 800 px wide, or on a touch screen under 500 px tall, the panel is a full-screen overlay. On a phone on its side the header also hides and the player fills the screen. The chip at the player's top left shows what Auto-Pause did.
  • Swipe left or right on the player for the next or previous item. On a touch screen, double-tap the player's left or right half to seek a video back or forward by the seek step, and keep tapping to seek again; a single tap then acts after a moment. J and L and double-taps show the seek indicator at that side. Favorite and Blacklist clear each other.
  • Ambient mode: a blurred, dimmed copy of the picture's colors fills the black space around it, drifting as the video plays. The Settings tab turns it off, and its Ambient mode settings set the update rate, fade time, blur, strength in each theme, and saturation.
  • Volume: the scroll wheel over the player and a two-finger drag on a touch screen change it by the volume step, and so do [ and ]; each shows the volume indicator, centered over the player, as M does, reading Muted while muted, which the slider and the Mute button don't. Mute moves the slider to 0 and unmuting brings the volume back. While muted, raising the volume unmutes and starts from 0, and lowering it does nothing. Lowering it to 0 mutes, and unmuting then brings back the volume from before that drag, notch, or key press. With Enhanced audio, on by default in Settings, every device plays through a Web Audio gain stage, started on the first tap, so iOS gets a volume slider too. The Mockup tab's stand-in sound plays with videos so the volume can be heard, and the Mockup tab can make the gain stage fail to show its notice. With Loudness normalization on, the stand-in sound follows each video's loudness against the baseline.
  • Autoplay has two modes in Settings: Normal, where photos advance after Advance after and videos play to the end, and Timer, where every item advances after it, a paused video holding the timer. With Loop on, the item repeats and nothing advances while Loop stops autoplay is on, its default; in Timer mode a video then repeats a stretch of Advance after seconds, from its start or from where a seek lands, shown faintly on the scrub bar. Turned off, autoplay goes on with Loop on, and a video shorter than Advance after repeats until it advances: at once in Timer mode, and once the repeat in progress finishes in Normal mode. A repeat never loads the item again.
  • The scrub bar and the volume slider are drawn by the page: orange up to the value, a slightly see-through dark gray after it, and a plain orange knob, with the pointing hand over them. Under a mouse, one grows in place, its track thicker and its knob full size, and a label above the pointer shows the time, or the volume, at that point; the scrub bar also fills light gray from the position up to a pointer ahead of it. A finger on one gives the same look and label, the label higher, without the light gray, until it lifts. The knob's box, which takes presses, stays finger-sized on a touch screen. The volume indicator over the player doesn't show for the slider, whose label is its readout.
  • Loop markers: a right-click on a video's scrub bar, or a long press on a touch screen, opens a menu that sets the loop's start or end at the time under the pointer, such as "Set loop start at 1:23", and clears both. I and O set them where the video is, and X clears them. Setting one turns Loop on, and turning Loop off clears them. The stretch between them shows faintly on the bar, as Timer mode's does, with a thin orange line at each marker, on top of the bar and a little taller than the stretch, that drags the marker, leaving the video where it is. The video repeats between them, and with only a start or an end set, from it to the video's end or from the video's start to it. While either is set, Loop treats the stretch as the whole video: it takes the place of Timer mode's stretch, and with Loop stops autoplay off, a stretch shorter than Advance after repeats until it advances. A seek past the end marker plays on to the video's end before going back to the start marker. The markers stay in order a second apart, so one set past the other clears it. Whenever they change, by the menu, I or O, or letting go of a drag, a video outside the stretch, before or after it, jumps to the start marker, and one inside it stays where it is. Another item, or the same one played again, starts without them.
  • On a photo that autoplay will advance, Play or Space pauses and resumes its timer, and dragging the scrub bar moves it. Otherwise both are disabled on a photo.
  • Library: the count line, such as "615 / 49,106 shown", controls that collapse, with Preset and Search sharing a row above Sort once the controls are 520 px wide, and stacked narrower, and no randomization dropdown, the preset dropdown reading "Preset: {name}" while closed, the preset dropdown, search, Sort, and its direction 32 px high, as the Filters and Random lines are, the filter summary line, the Random line under it, which always shows, names the Randomization tab's options, such as "Random: Shuffle · Spread by folder", or "Pure random" with every option off, and opens that tab, both lines ending with a chevron, the playing tile's icon, the favorite and blacklist icons, the Rating sort, where an unrated file counts as 0 stars, the Date added and Date created sorts, placeholder tiles while a page loads, and file names on tiles as a setting. Selection works as in Google Photos: start it with the check at a tile's top left when hovering it, a Shift, Ctrl, or Cmd+click, or Select in a tile's menu; then a click adds or removes a tile, Shift+click adds a range, and deselecting the last tile, Clear selection, Esc while the Library tab shows, or a new search or filter ends it. The bar reads Select all, Clear selection, the count, such as "15 / 615 selected", and Selection actions, its menu. Edit tags opens the Tags tab on the selected items. A right-click on a tile, or a long press on a touch screen, opens the same menu at the pointer, acting on the selection when the tile is selected and otherwise on that tile alone, which shows an orange ring, keeping the selection, and adds Copy path and Show in File Manager while it acts on one item, whose Rate… starts at that file's rating. While the tile isn't selected, the menu starts with Select. Each item has an icon, an add or remove shows only while it applies, and Clear playback stats is disabled with its reason when there are none to clear.
  • Library zoom: Ctrl+scroll over the grid, which a laptop trackpad pinch also sends, and a two-finger pinch on a touch screen scale the grid around the pointer or the pinch's center while the gesture lasts. When it settles, the rows reflow once to the new row height, from 120 to 300 px, the tile under the pointer keeps its vertical place, and each tile moves from where it showed to its new place; with reduced motion they take their places at once. A change that comes during the gesture, such as a filter or a favorite, shows when it settles, in that one reflow. Ctrl+scroll never zooms the page.
  • Filter, Tags, and their dialogs as approved so far, with Favorites and Blacklisted each set to only or excluded, plus the browser-playable option, Only unrated, and Min Rating, set with the rating's stars; Only unrated with a minimum is flagged, as Favorites only with Blacklisted only is. The Date Filter's Added and Created each take Any time, Last 7, 30, or 90 days, This year, or Custom range, whose From and To come from the device's date picker and start on today; an emptied date, a date outside 1970 to 2099, or a To before its From, is flagged. The device's time zone stands in for the server's, and the Recently added preset holds Added Last 30 days. Pick an .mkv, .avi, or .wmv tile, or the .heic photo, to see the format notice; autoplay, Next, Previous, and random picks skip them with a popup, up to ten in a row. The Mockup tab's stalled drive or share shows the couldn't-load notice, and the player stops.
  • Tag categories start collapsed and remember which are open, separately in the Tags tab and the Filter tab. A category's header, expanded or collapsed, shows how many of its tags are in use and, with unsaved changes, the orange dot; adding a tag to a category, or moving one into it, opens it.
  • Auto Tag opens from the Tags tab over the whole page. Its scan runs for about 5 seconds, then lists the tags file names match, and its own Apply applies the checked changes.
  • Profiles: the header's profile button, before the admin icon, shows the active profile's colored circle and first letter, and opens the picker, styled like the bell's list: the default profile, Main, first, then the others alphabetically, each with an edit icon, and Add Profile at the bottom. Choosing one switches this device to it, after "Discard changes?" while the Filter or Tags tab holds unsaved changes; the playing file carries on. Add Profile and Edit Profile are one dialog with General (name and color), Sources, Tags, and Presets tabs, and the default profile's has only its name and color. The color button opens the app's own color picker, the same on every device: a saturation and brightness square, a hue slider, the palette's colors as quick picks, and the hex field with a random button. In a profile, the Library, Filter, Tags, Stats, and Randomization tabs, Auto Tag, and the popups about another device's changes cover only its sources and the tags, categories, and presets it shows, and each profile has its own default filter, which Update writes while Choose Preset is on None. Creating a tag, category, or preset asks "Only this profile" or "All profiles" once a second profile exists, and Edit Tag, Edit Category, and Edit Preset have Show in.
  • Right-click menus, in place of the browser's own, which shows only in text fields: on the player's picture, the header's file name, the Stats tab's file name, path, and tags, the Tags tab's tags and categories, the Filter tab's preset rows and sources, the profile picker's rows, the bell's entries, the popups, Auto Tag's file rows, and the Settings tab's Client and Session IDs. On a touch screen, a long press opens them, except on buttons and drag handles. The element a menu acts on is tinted while it is open, and a menu on the playing file closes when another file plays. Edit Tag, Edit Category, and Edit Preset open from their menus' Edit…, so tags, categories, and preset rows have no edit button. A tag shows only the buttons that change something: + while it isn't on the item, − while it is, both only on a tag some of several selected items have, and none with nothing playing. Filter by this tag adds the tag to the Filter tab's filter and opens the tab, for Apply, and waits for Save on a new or renamed tag. Show in File Manager shows with the Mockup tab's "This browser is on the server machine".
  • Stats: the current file, with its path, whose menu copies it, and its tags below its stats, and the library. Click the file name in the header to open it. A video's Adjustment shows what loudness normalization applies, with “(limited by peak)” when the video's peak holds a boost back, or Off while it or Enhanced audio is off. True peak is the loudness scan's, Not measured, or N/A, and Last played is the time before this play. Added and Created follow it. All three say how long ago, from Just now through minutes, hours, days, and months to years, and the header's Show exact times, orange while on and remembered on this device, shows them as YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS in 24-hour local time. The Mockup tab's Stats load shows a failed load.
  • Rating: the current file's 5-star rating, with half stars, on its own line in Stats. Click or tap a star's left half for half a star less, or drag along the stars on a touch screen, where a touch on them never scrolls the tab. The first star's left edge clears it, with no Clear button, and so does a drag off the stars' left end. A half star is orange only on its filled half. A mouse over the stars previews the rating in place of the set one. Empty stars are the panel's text color. A popup says each change, and the Library's Rated card counts the rated files.
  • The player's rating: the star left of Favorite turns orange, with the rating in a badge, while the playing file is rated. Press it for the same stars at the player's center, white where empty, which set the rating as in Stats. A click, tap, or drag that sets it closes them, and so does Escape, a press elsewhere, another file playing, or a volume change; the rating shortcuts change the rating without closing them. Shift+1 to Shift+5 rate the playing file, Shift+0 clears its rating, and - and = step it by half a star.
  • Randomization: one Random with options that combine, which apply at once and are kept on this device. The Shuffle cycle card at the top, which opens and closes and starts open, shows this device's files picked this cycle, those eligible now under the current filter, and those remaining, and notes when Shuffle is off. Shuffle, with Remember plays for (1, 7, 30, or 90 days, or until everything has played) and Reset now; Spread by folder; Spread by tags, by All tags or By category, with a checkbox for each tag category, flagged while none is chosen; and Favor less played, newly added, newly created, and higher rated. Shuffle on and Spread by folder are the defaults. The mockup's picks stay uniform, since its 72 items are too few to show the options' effect, apart from Shuffle, which picks nothing twice until every eligible file has been picked.
  • Settings, in sections: Playback (autoplay mode, Advance after, Loop stops autoplay, seek step, Play behavior), Audio (volume step, Enhanced audio, Loudness normalization with its target, boost limit, and Allow limiting, and Compression with its strength, Light, Medium, Strong, or Custom with its values), Player controls (when they hide), Appearance (theme, ambient mode and its settings, file names on tiles), Notifications (whether popups show, and under Popup settings, for which kinds, where, whether in fullscreen, and for how long, and Show seconds for the bell's times), Panel (side, Auto-Pause), Keyboard shortcuts (collapsed; H opens it), and Diagnostics. Checkboxes, radio buttons, and the Settings tab's sliders use the orange accent; the player's sliders are drawn by the page.
  • No focus ring shows anywhere, as in the app. Text fields, dropdowns, and editable text keep the keys pressed in them. Everywhere else a key does only what a shortcut binds it to, and nothing otherwise, so Tab, Enter, and the arrow and page keys move, press, and scroll nothing; keys with Ctrl, Alt, or Meta and F1 to F12 stay the browser's, and an in-app dialog takes every key. A shortcut takes focus off the control it was pressed on, and a dropdown gives up focus after a pick.
  • Keyboard shortcuts work: H (shows them), K or Space, J, L, arrows, R, F, B, A, M, comma and period (one frame, while paused), I, O, and X (loop markers), [ and ] (volume), Shift+0 to Shift+5, - and = (rating), 1 to 6 (the tabs, in order), T, S, P, and Shift+F.
  • The Mockup tab on the left edge sets the situation: the server reconnecting, not answering, running without a library, failing the compatibility check, or with an empty library; pairing asked for (a token of "wrong" fails); the server machine; a read-only volume; and a phone on its side. It also starts and ends each lasting state, a newer build on the server among them, sends a test notification of each kind and one kept from yesterday, a favorite, a rating, and preset changes from another device, another device deleting this device's profile, and a runtime config failure, and can hide every mockup note to show screens as they will ship.

admin_panel_settingsAdmin view

  • The full-page view the header's admin icon opens: server and updates, web access, control, refresh, library transfer, backups, sources, duplicates, the testing suite, clients, the Log Viewer, and events. Daily backups start off, at 0 days, and the restore list shows daily backups only once they're on. Backups, connected clients, and the Log Viewer's lines show their time, "HH:MM" today and with "MM-DD" before it for another day. An import or a restore first backs up the current library, listed first as "(before import)" or "(before restore)". Apply & Restart brings the server back on the new version, and its bell's Now then lists the update notice, whose tap reloads the page. Adding a source starts a library refresh, which brings in its files, and restarts one that runs.
  • Duplicate review opens within it. The control token gate shows first from another machine.
  • It has no player, so its bell is at the end of its own bar, which stays on a phone on its side; its acknowledgments pop up, and Refresh Now shows its stage, percent, and progress line in its own card, which otherwise shows the last refresh's time and summary, or that the server has run none since it started.
  • Refresh Status, the import's upload progress, and its notice for a file that isn't a library. The mockup bar also shows a dev run's version line and a phone on its side, where the admin bar stays.

restart_altRecovery page

  • The server's own plain page for when the WebUI's files are missing or broken: restart, stop, updates, and the newest log lines, behind the control token from another machine.

errorField validation pattern

  • Each state of the durations (parsed strictly), the Favorites and Blacklisted pair, the Only unrated and Min Rating pair, the custom date range, the new preset name, and the Edit Tag and Edit Category names.
  • Apply when the field that is not valid is out of view.

Numbers set with these mockups

Panel widthMinimum 360 px, default 420 px, maximum whatever leaves the player 400 px wide.
Full-screen overlayBelow 800 px wide (360 + handle + 400 + page padding), or on a touch screen under 500 px tall, which is a phone on its side.
Default sideRight. Each device can choose Left in the Settings tab's Panel section.
Library tilesRows aim for 160 px, from 100 to 240. Ctrl+scroll or a pinch zooms the aim from 120 to 300 px, the range scales with it, and each device remembers it. Tiles sit 2 px apart while the rows aim for 120 to 179 px, 3 px for 180 to 239 px, and 4 px for 240 to 300 px, across a row and between rows, in whole device pixels. Every tile has 4 px corners, and a selected tile's thumbnail is inset 8 px on every side. A wheel notch zooms 10%, eased over a few frames; a gesture settles 200 ms after the wheel stops or as the fingers lift, and the tiles then move to their new places over 220 ms.
Layout measurementsThe grid's figures stay 3 seconds after a zoom settles, with the gap the rows draw, on whole device pixels, to two decimals. The corner box sits at the bottom left of what shows on screen, 8 px inside the safe areas.
PopupsAt most 3 at once, each for 3, 5 (the default), or 10 seconds.
Notifications listThe last 50 messages, kept on the device with what was read; a reset of the device's settings keeps them.
Seek indicatorA double-tap's taps within 300 ms of each other. The total starts over a second after the last seek, as the indicator fades out. It fades in over 120 ms, its chevron sliding out over 300 ms, and out over 250 ms.
Player indicatorLoading… after half a second. Below the corner buttons on a player under 600 px wide; above that, at most the player's width less 440 px, or 336 px while the panel is on the left, so it clears them.
Player slidersThe track 4 px thick, 6 px under a mouse or a finger; the knob 10 px, 16 px under a mouse or a finger. The knob's box, which takes presses, 16 px, or 28 px on a touch screen, where the sliders are 32 px tall within the 24 px row. The track, with rounded ends, runs from the knob's center at 0 to its center at the end, and keeps its length as it grows. Its fills follow its rounded ends, and the loop and Timer stretches round at them too. The label is a 22 px pill. The label sits 6 px above the slider, or 28 px above it for a finger.
Loop markersAt least 1 second apart, and each at least 1 second from the video's other end. The stretch is 14 px tall, and a marker a 3 px line 18 px tall, both centered on the bar, and takes presses in a 24 px square around it, or 36 px on a touch screen.
ProfilesA profile's circle is 32 px in the header and the picker, its letter white where white reaches 3:1 on its color, as large bold text needs, and dark otherwise. The color picker's square is 200 px, with a 32 px hue slider beside it. The palette's 7 quick picks are red, the brand orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet. A new profile starts on a random color, any hex value, at least 0.12 apart in OKLab from every other profile's color, and the random button picks another that far from them and from the one shown; after 20 tries without one, it takes the most distinct.
Long press500 ms, cancelled by moving 8 px, a second finger, or lifting first.
RatingEach star 36 px wide and 30 px tall, its icon's size, with the first star's left 8 px clearing the rating. Changes from the rating shortcuts, - and = and Shift+0 to Shift+5, are sent once the keys pause for 500 ms.
