ReelRoulette design mockup

The app's whole visible UI, approved before the v0.15.0 milestones that build it and kept matching the app as each one ships. Every page works at desktop and phone widths, from about 425 px up, below which the player's transport row would wrap, and follows the system light or dark theme. Magenta dashed boxes are mockup notes. Every screen here is approved, and each decision is recorded in the milestone that builds it.

dock_to_rightMain page

  • Nothing plays until a tap or Play starts a random pick. Previous and Next step through what played, and Next and autoplay pick at random at its end. R and the Random button pick from anywhere in it, adding the pick at the end or, with Random behavior's Insert, right after the current item. Each skips a missing file or one this browser can't play, saying so in a popup.
  • No status line. Lasting states (reconnecting, a server that fails the compatibility check or runs without a library, loading, and a library refresh) show one at a time on the player's indicator. One-off messages pop up briefly, three at most. The bell at the player's top left, which shows and hides with its other controls, lists recent messages, the in-app notices included, and every lasting state under Now; its badge counts what didn't pop up, and the bell is orange while there is any.
  • The player's one panel button opens the panel on its last tab, and closes it. It sits on the panel's side, at the top right, or just right of the bell while the panel is on the left. The rating's star, Favorite, and Blacklist stay on the player; the seek row ends with Mute and the volume slider. The panel's tabs are, in order, Library, Tags, Stats, Filter, Randomization, and Settings.
  • The player's controls don't appear, hide, or move on their own; a control may hide only because of a user setting, as the volume slider does on iOS while Enhanced audio is off.
  • Drag the handle between the player and the panel. Open or closed, side, width, and last tab survive a reload.
  • Below 800 px wide, or on a touch screen under 500 px tall, the panel is a full-screen overlay. On a phone on its side the header also hides and the player fills the screen. The chip at the player's top left shows what Auto-Pause did.
  • Swipe left or right on the player for the next or previous item. On a touch screen, double-tap the player's left or right half to seek a video back or forward by the seek step, and keep tapping to seek again; a single tap then acts after a moment. J and L and double-taps show the seek indicator at that side. Favorite and Blacklist clear each other.
  • Ambient mode: a blurred, dimmed copy of the picture's colors fills the black space around it, drifting as the video plays. The Settings tab turns it off, and its Ambient mode settings set the update rate, fade time, blur, strength in each theme, and saturation.
  • Volume: the scroll wheel over the player and a two-finger drag on a touch screen change it by the volume step, and so do [ and ]; each shows the volume indicator, centered over the player, as M does, reading Muted while muted, which the slider and the Mute button don't. Mute moves the slider to 0 and unmuting brings the volume back. While muted, raising the volume unmutes and starts from 0, and lowering it does nothing. Lowering it to 0 mutes, and unmuting then brings back the volume from before that drag, notch, or key press. With Enhanced audio, on by default in Settings, every device plays through a Web Audio gain stage, started on the first tap, so iOS gets a volume slider too. The Mockup tab's stand-in sound plays with videos so the volume can be heard, and the Mockup tab can make the gain stage fail to show its notice. With Loudness normalization on, the stand-in sound follows each video's loudness against the baseline.
  • Autoplay has two modes in Settings: Normal, where photos advance after Advance after and videos play to the end, and Timer, where every item advances after it, a paused video holding the timer. With Loop on, the item repeats and nothing advances while Loop stops autoplay is on, its default; in Timer mode a video then repeats a stretch of Advance after seconds, from its start or from where a seek lands, shown faintly on the scrub bar. Turned off, autoplay goes on with Loop on, and a video shorter than Advance after repeats until it advances: at once in Timer mode, and once the repeat in progress finishes in Normal mode. A repeat never loads the item again.
  • On a photo that autoplay will advance, Play or Space pauses and resumes its timer, and dragging the scrub bar moves it. Otherwise both are disabled on a photo.
  • Library: controls that collapse, with Preset and Search sharing a row above Sort and no randomization dropdown, the filter summary line, the Random line under it, which always shows, names the Randomization tab's options, such as "Random: Shuffle · Spread by folder", or "Pure random" with every option off, and opens that tab, the playing tile's icon, the favorite and blacklist icons, the Rating sort, where an unrated file counts as 0 stars, the Date added and Date created sorts, placeholder tiles while a page loads, and file names on tiles as a setting. Selection works as in Google Photos: start it with the check at a tile's top left when hovering it, or a long press on a touch screen; then a click adds or removes a tile, Shift+click adds a range, and deselecting the last tile ends it. Edit tags opens the Tags tab on the selected items.
  • Library zoom: Ctrl+scroll over the grid, which a laptop trackpad pinch also sends, and a two-finger pinch on a touch screen scale the grid around the pointer or the pinch's center while the gesture lasts. When it settles, the rows reflow once to the new row height, from 100 to 300 px, the tile under the pointer keeps its vertical place, and each tile moves from where it showed to its new place; with reduced motion they take their places at once. A change that comes during the gesture, such as a filter or a favorite, shows when it settles, in that one reflow. Ctrl+scroll never zooms the page.
  • Filter, Tags, and their dialogs as approved so far, with Favorites and Blacklisted each set to only or excluded, plus the browser-playable option, Only unrated, and Min Rating, set with the rating's stars; Only unrated with a minimum is flagged, as Favorites only with Blacklisted only is. The Date Filter's Added and Created each take Any time, Last 7, 30, or 90 days, This year, or Custom range, whose From and To come from the device's date picker and start on today; an emptied date, a date outside 1970 to 2099, or a To before its From, is flagged. The device's time zone stands in for the server's, and the Recently added preset holds Added Last 30 days. Pick an .mkv, .avi, or .wmv tile, or the .heic photo, to see the format notice; autoplay, Next, Previous, and random picks skip them with a popup, up to ten in a row. The Mockup tab's stalled drive or share shows the couldn't-load notice, and the player stops.
  • Tag categories start collapsed and remember which are open, separately in the Tags tab and the Filter tab. A category's header, expanded or collapsed, shows how many of its tags are in use and, with unsaved changes, the orange dot; adding a tag to a category, or moving one into it, opens it.
  • Auto Tag opens from the Tags tab over the whole page. Its scan runs for about 5 seconds, then lists the tags file names match, and its own Apply applies the checked changes.
  • Stats: the current file, with Show in File Manager or Copy Path as an icon button beside its path and its tags below its stats, and the library. Click the file name in the header to open it. A video's Adjustment shows what loudness normalization applies, with “(limited by peak)” when the video's peak holds a boost back, or Off while it or Enhanced audio is off. True peak is the loudness scan's, Not measured, or N/A, and Last played is the time before this play. Added and Created follow it. All three say how long ago, from Just now through minutes, hours, days, and months to years, and the header's Show exact times, orange while on and remembered on this device, shows them as YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS in 24-hour local time. The Mockup tab's Stats load shows a failed load.
  • Rating: the current file's 5-star rating, with half stars, on its own line in Stats. Click or tap a star's left half for half a star less, or drag along the stars on a touch screen, where a touch on them never scrolls the tab. The first star's left edge clears it, with no Clear button, and so does a drag off the stars' left end. A half star is orange only on its filled half. A mouse over the stars previews the rating in place of the set one. Empty stars are the panel's text color. A popup says each change, and the Library's Rated card counts the rated files.
  • The player's rating: the star left of Favorite turns orange, with the rating in a badge, while the playing file is rated. Press it for the same stars at the player's center, white where empty, which set the rating as in Stats. A click, tap, or drag that sets it closes them, and so does Escape, a press elsewhere, another file playing, or a volume change; the rating shortcuts change the rating without closing them. Shift+1 to Shift+5 rate the playing file, Shift+0 clears its rating, and - and = step it by half a star.
  • Randomization: one Random with options that combine, which apply at once and are kept on this device. The Shuffle cycle card at the top, which opens and closes and starts open, shows this device's files picked this cycle, those eligible now under the current filter, and those remaining, and notes when Shuffle is off. Shuffle, with Remember plays for (1, 7, 30, or 90 days, or until everything has played) and Reset now; Spread by folder; Spread by tags, by All tags or By category, with a checkbox for each tag category, flagged while none is chosen; Favor less played, newly added, newly created, and higher rated; and Random behavior, where Jump adds a pick from the Random button or R at the end of the history and Insert puts it right after the current item. Shuffle on and Spread by folder are the defaults. The mockup's picks stay uniform, since its 72 items are too few to show the options' effect, apart from Shuffle, which picks nothing twice until every eligible file has been picked.
  • Settings, in sections: Playback (autoplay mode, Advance after, Loop stops autoplay, seek step), Audio (volume step, Enhanced audio, Loudness normalization with its target, boost limit, and Allow limiting, and Compression with its strength, Light, Medium, Strong, or Custom with its values), Player controls (when they hide), Appearance (theme, ambient mode and its settings, file names on tiles), Notifications (whether popups show, and under Popup settings, for which kinds, where, whether in fullscreen, and for how long), Panel (side, Auto-Pause), Keyboard shortcuts (collapsed; H opens it), and Diagnostics. Checkboxes, radio buttons, and sliders use the orange accent.
  • No focus ring shows anywhere, as in the app. Text fields, dropdowns, and editable text keep the keys pressed in them. Everywhere else a key does only what a shortcut binds it to, and nothing otherwise, so Tab, Enter, and the arrow and page keys move, press, and scroll nothing; keys with Ctrl, Alt, or Meta and F1 to F12 stay the browser's, and an in-app dialog takes every key. A shortcut takes focus off the control it was pressed on, and a dropdown gives up focus after a pick.
  • Keyboard shortcuts work: H (shows them), K or Space, J, L, arrows, R, F, B, A, M, comma and period (one frame, while paused), [ and ] (volume), Shift+0 to Shift+5, - and = (rating), 1 to 6 (the tabs, in order), T, S, P, and Shift+F.
  • The Mockup tab on the left edge sets the situation: the server reconnecting, not answering, running without a library, failing the compatibility check, or with an empty library; pairing asked for (a token of "wrong" fails); the server machine; a read-only volume; and a phone on its side. It also starts and ends each lasting state, sends a test notification of each kind, a favorite, a rating, and preset changes from another device, and a runtime config failure, and can hide every mockup note to show screens as they will ship.

admin_panel_settingsAdmin view

  • The full-page view the header's admin icon opens: server and updates, web access, control, refresh, library transfer, backups, sources, duplicates, the testing suite, clients, the Log Viewer, and events. Daily backups start off, at 0 days, and the restore list shows daily backups only once they're on. An import or a restore first backs up the current library, listed first as "(before import)" or "(before restore)". Adding a source starts a library refresh, which brings in its files, and restarts one that runs.
  • Duplicate review opens within it. The control token gate shows first from another machine.
  • It has no player, so its bell is at the end of its own bar, which stays on a phone on its side; its acknowledgments pop up, and Refresh Now shows its stage and progress in its own card.
  • Refresh Status, the import's upload progress, and its notice for a file that isn't a library. The mockup bar also shows a dev run's version line and a phone on its side, where the admin bar stays.

restart_altRecovery page

  • The server's own plain page for when the WebUI's files are missing or broken: restart, stop, updates, and the newest log lines, behind the control token from another machine.

errorField validation pattern

  • Each state of the durations (parsed strictly), the Favorites and Blacklisted pair, the Only unrated and Min Rating pair, the custom date range, the new preset name, and the Edit Tag and Edit Category names.
  • Apply when the field that is not valid is out of view.

Numbers set with these mockups

Panel widthMinimum 360 px, default 420 px, maximum whatever leaves the player 400 px wide.
Full-screen overlayBelow 800 px wide (360 + handle + 400 + page padding), or on a touch screen under 500 px tall, which is a phone on its side.
Default sideRight, until the Settings tab lets each device choose.
Library tilesRows aim for 160 px, from 100 to 240. Ctrl+scroll or a pinch zooms the aim from 100 to 300 px, the range scales with it, and each device remembers it. Tiles sit 1 px apart, across a row and between rows, at every row height. A wheel notch zooms 15%, eased over a few frames; a gesture settles 200 ms after the wheel stops or as the fingers lift, and the tiles then move to their new places over 220 ms.
PopupsAt most 3 at once, each for 3, 5 (the default), or 10 seconds.
Notifications listThe last 50 messages, for the page's life.
Seek indicatorA double-tap's taps within 300 ms of each other. The total starts over a second after the last seek, as the indicator fades out. It fades in over 120 ms, its chevron sliding out over 300 ms, and out over 250 ms.
Player indicatorLoading… after half a second. Below the corner buttons on a player under 600 px wide; above that, at most the player's width less 440 px, or 336 px while the panel is on the left, so it clears them.
RatingEach star 36 px wide and 30 px tall, its icon's size, with the first star's left 8 px clearing the rating. Changes from the rating shortcuts, - and = and Shift+0 to Shift+5, are sent once the keys pause for 500 ms.
