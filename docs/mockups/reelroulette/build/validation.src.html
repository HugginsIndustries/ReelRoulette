Field validation pattern

Every field below is live. Point at a red icon, or put the cursor in a flagged field, to see its tooltip; on a touch screen tap the icon. Magenta dashed boxes are mockup notes, not part of the design.

Disabled or held

A field that may sit empty, such as a name typed for an action, is never flagged while it is empty: the action that needs it is disabled, dimmed, and pressing it does nothing. A field that must always hold a value for the app to work, such as Advance after in Settings, is flagged when it is emptied, so the user sees it. Any other problem is flagged in its field, with the red icon and the tooltip. An action whose fields all sit right next to it, a field beside its button or the fields of the dialog it is in, is disabled while one of them has a problem, with no icon on the button, since the field says why. An action that uses a field elsewhere, such as Apply, is held instead: its label dims, a red badge shows on its top-right corner without changing its width, and pressing it moves to the field, as the last section shows. The Filter tab's Apply, an icon button and the one held action with an orange dot, dims its icon and turns its dot red instead of showing the badge, and Add Preset and Choose Preset's Update, icon buttons without that dot, dim their icons and show a red dot where Apply's sits. Each red icon, in a field or on the corner of a held action or a Filter section's tab, a held action's red dot, and the tooltip fade in and out, and Apply's dot changes color, over about 250 ms, the icons keeping their place while they fade out; with reduced motion they show and hide at once.

A setting's field (Settings tab)

A setting applies without Save, and a field of one, such as Advance after, must always hold a value. A valid value is saved only on Enter or when the field loses focus, so a value passed through on the way, such as the “1” of “120”, or of “15” being cleared with Backspace, is never saved. A value that is not valid, an emptied field included, is flagged as it is typed and stays flagged while the field has focus. When the field loses focus with such a value, or its tab hides or the panel closes, it shows the value saved again, unflagged. A save that changes the value shows a green check where the red icon sits for about two seconds, fading in and out as the red icon does, and screen readers hear “Saved”; nothing shows when the value stays as it was, or when a value that is not valid goes back to the one saved. Every typed field in the Settings tab works this way: Advance after, Hide after, and Loudness normalization's maximum reduction, maximum boost, and target. Choices such as Side show their change at once and have no check.

Duration (Filter tab, General)

An empty field means no limit, and its placeholder says so: “No minimum” or “No maximum”. It is never flagged. Anything other than whole MM:SS or HH:MM:SS (seconds under 60, and minutes under 60 in HH:MM:SS) or a number of seconds is flagged as it is typed: “1:7x”, “12abc”, and “1:75” all are.

Favorites and Blacklisted (Filter tab, General)

Checked as a pair. No item is both a favorite and blacklisted, so Favorites only with Blacklisted only matches nothing and is flagged as it is chosen; every other pairing is allowed. One icon sits just after the Blacklisted dropdown. Both dropdowns are marked invalid and described by its tooltip, and focusing either one shows it. Apply, Add Preset, and Update preset can't proceed until either changes, and pressing one moves to the Blacklisted dropdown.

New preset name (Filter tab, Presets)

Required, but an empty name is never flagged: Add Preset is disabled until the field has a name. A name a saved preset already has, ignoring case, is flagged as it is typed, and Add Preset, which sits beside the field, is disabled too, without an icon. A problem in a General field, such as a duration that is not valid, holds Add Preset instead, as it holds Apply. Saved presets here: Favorites, Short clips, Long videos.

New tag name (Tags tab)

An empty name is never flagged: Add tag is disabled until the field has a name. A name another tag already has, ignoring case, is flagged as it is typed, and Add tag, which sits beside the field, is disabled too. An existing tag goes on the item with its chip's Add instead. Tags here: Beach, Mountains, Alice.

Edit Tag dialog: tag name

The name opens filled in. Emptying it disables Save, without a flag. It can take another tag's name, since the server merges the two tags. Delete asks first.

Edit Category dialog: category name

The category's Edit button opens this, and Delete asks first. A name another category has, ignoring case, is flagged as it is typed, and Save is disabled, without an icon, since the field says why. Emptying the name disables Save too, without a flag. New Category and Edit Preset check their names the same way. Categories here: People, Places, Events; editing People.

Apply while the field that is not valid is out of view

The minimum duration below is not valid and sits at the bottom of General, out of view. The General tab carries the red badge on its corner, so the problem can be found from Tags or Presets too.

Apply's icon dims and its orange dot turns red. Pressing it applies nothing: it switches to General, scrolls the field into view, puts the cursor in it, and shows its tooltip.
