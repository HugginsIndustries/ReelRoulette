Responsive layout and panels: mockups

For approval before code. Both pages work at desktop and phone widths and follow the system light or dark theme.

dock_to_rightPanel layout

  • The player's one panel button opens the panel on its last tab, and closes it. Favorite and Blacklist stay on the player.
  • Drag the handle between the player and the panel, or focus it and use the arrow keys. Open or closed, side, width, and last tab survive a reload.
  • Below 800 px wide, or on a touch screen under 500 px tall, the panel is a full-screen overlay. The chip at the player's top left shows what Auto-Pause did.
  • Tags tab: tap a category's name to collapse it; categories reorder by their handle; chips and categories open an edit dialog that holds Delete.
  • Unsaved Filter and Tags changes survive closing the panel and switching tabs; an orange dot marks their tab, and the player's panel button, until they are applied, saved, canceled, or discarded.
  • The Tags tab shows the playing item's tags and follows it. Add or remove a tag, then press Next: the tab stays on its item with an “Editing tags for …” line until you Save, or Refresh and discard.
  • Filter tab, Presets: presets reorder by their handle, and Edit opens an Edit Preset dialog with the name and Delete.
  • Every dialog is an in-app dialog. Delete asks in a second dialog stacked above, with Cancel focused so Enter never deletes; Escape closes only the top one.
  • Every third item is a photo, so Next moves between photos and videos. On a photo the scrub bar stays, disabled, and fills as the photo's timer runs while Autoplay is on.
  • The Mockup tab on the left edge switches the side, Auto-Pause, theme, and what later milestones add.

errorField validation pattern

  • Each state of the durations (parsed strictly), the new preset name, and the Edit Tag and Edit Category names, with what a screen reader gets.
  • Apply when the field that is not valid is out of view.
